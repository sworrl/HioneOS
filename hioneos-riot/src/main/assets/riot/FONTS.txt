Riot mode fonts and art
=======================

font_large, font_medium, font_small ("Riot Sans")
  Bitmap sheets rasterized from Liberation Sans Regular with FreeType's monochrome renderer,
  cropped and respaced for a 240 x 160 LCD by tools/make_riot_assets.py. This is a Modified
  Version of the Liberation Fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, renamed as the license
  requires. It is distributed under the same license, copied below.

  Liberation Fonts:
    Digitized data copyright (c) 2010 Google Corporation with Reserved Font Arimo, Tinos and Cousine.
    Copyright (c) 2012 Red Hat, Inc. with Reserved Font Name Liberation.

font_tiny, icons/play, pause, stop, speaker, check, usb, pointer, lock
  Drawn by hand for this project, pixel by pixel (see tools/make_riot_assets.py).

logo_rioriot.png, icons/usb_connected.png, icons/battery_*.png
  Bitmaps recovered from the Rio Riot 1.25 firmware image (see hioneos/docs/rio-riot-firmware.md
  and tools/import_firmware_bitmaps.py). The logo and USB picture are used as recovered; the
  battery gauges are rebuilt from the recovered gauge strip. logo_rioriot.png is the only logo
  file: delete or replace it and the boot and locked screens simply draw without it.

A fan tribute. Not affiliated with or endorsed by SonicBlue, D&M Holdings, or Rio.
